** Variables 
	Number of arenas = 2
	max player per arena = 4
	Game time per slot = 30 minutes 
	
** Bookig Description
	For the opening hours described above, each arena can be booked on the basis of 30 minutes for each booking slot. 
	For each booking the customer chooses which mode to play for his booking group:  escape room or shooter game ? 
	then based on his choice he gets to choose among the available games of each category.  Each game is presented with its name and promo photo with hyper link. 
	if the photo is cliked, a new tab appears with promo video of the game

	the customer has to give how many players are in the group (up to 4 player shall be in one arena) more then book 2 arenas if available
	each arena has 4 open slots to book every 30 minutes. 
	the price of 1 slot per player (30 minutes) is 15 € in the middle of the week.  and 20 € on Saturday. 
	The person who books shall give in his name and email address. the system stores these
	after payment, the customer receives a confirmation email. And explanation of the game, the rules , reminder to be there 10 minutes in advance.
